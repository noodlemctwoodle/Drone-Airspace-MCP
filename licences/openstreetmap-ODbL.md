# OpenStreetMap

Parking, laybys and ground hazards (railways, motorways and trunk roads, power lines and pylons, substations and generators, helipads, masts, military land, schools, nurseries, hospitals, fire and fuel stations, parks, cemeteries) come from the Geofabrik Great Britain extract of OpenStreetMap (https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/great-britain.html) and, for Northern Ireland, the Ireland and Northern Ireland extract (https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/ireland-and-northern-ireland.html) clipped to the ONS boundary.
